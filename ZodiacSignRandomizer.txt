THE CONCEPT

Create a highly photorealistic, high-fashion editorial photograph of the exact same adult woman embodying one of the twelve zodiac signs — a different sign every generation, each rendered as a full dramatic persona with its own color world, mood, and staged scene, drawing on that sign's traditional astrological associations. This is the flagship of the series: maximum drama, bold sign-specific makeup, and the sign's own symbol worked visibly into her outfit every time.

WHO SHE IS — LOCKED, NEVER RANDOMIZED

Face comes from the attached reference photo and does not move. Preserve exactly: face shape, bone structure, natural facial asymmetry, eye shape and spacing and color, nose shape, lips, cupid's bow, jawline, cheekbones, skin texture, pores, fine lines. No beautifying, no reshaping, no symmetry correction, no age change, no generic-AI-face substitution. She must be immediately, unmistakably recognizable as herself in every image.

CURVY BODY LOCK: She has a defined, naturally curvy hourglass figure with a full, heavy bust, a noticeably cinched waist that sits clearly narrower than her bust and hips, and wide, rounded hips that flare out from the waist. Her thighs are full, soft, and shapely, touching at the top, with curved calves. Her upper arms are soft and rounded, her shoulders are feminine and slightly sloped, and her stomach is soft rather than flat or toned. Her body has a smooth, continuous curve from bust to waist to hip, with real softness and natural weight throughout, like a real woman rather than an idealized model. These proportions stay consistent from every angle, including close-ups, seated and reclining poses, side profiles, and overhead shots, and are identical across every generation. Her waist-to-hip curve must stay clearly visible in every image, even in flowing gowns, structured armor-like pieces, or dramatic trains and capes. Never make her thin, petite, slim, lean, athletic, muscular, straight-bodied, or boyish, and never shrink her bust, hips, thighs, or arms.

THE ZODIAC ENGINE — SEEDED SELECTION, DO THIS BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

Do not choose a sign directly. Choose a number first, then map it to a sign, and never override the result.

Step 1 — Get a seed. If you can access the current time, take the current minute (0–59) and add the current second (0–59) if you know it. If you cannot access the time, silently generate a random three-digit number between 100 and 999 whose three digits are all different, and add its digits together.

Step 2 — Take that total, divide by 12, and keep only the remainder. Add 1. You now have a number from 1 to 12.

Step 3 — Map it: 1 = Aries, 2 = Taurus, 3 = Gemini, 4 = Cancer, 5 = Leo, 6 = Virgo, 7 = Libra, 8 = Scorpio, 9 = Sagittarius, 10 = Capricorn, 11 = Aquarius, 12 = Pisces.

Step 4 — Commit to that sign. Do not swap it for one that "fits the drama better." Leo and Aries are known over-picked defaults for this kind of prompt — if the procedure lands there, fine, but never pick them by instinct, and never skip the procedure.

WHAT NEVER LOOKS FAKE

Every material has to survive close inspection: individual eyelash and brow hairs, visible skin pores, real hair strands, correct hands and fingernails, real fabric behavior matched to the sign's world (velvet has depth, metallics reflect true light, sheer fabric is genuinely translucent, embroidery sits with real thread texture). Environmental effects — fire glow, water reflection, moonlight, neon — must illuminate her skin and outfit with physically accurate color and falloff. Nothing reads as CGI, plastic, or illustrated, however dramatic the scene gets.

HOW SHE'S PHOTOGRAPHED

Expression is dramatic and powerful, but always flattering — never awkward or unflattering. Select one, matched to the sign's mood: a fierce, commanding stare; a sultry half-lidded gaze; a regal, composed expression; a knowing smirk; an intense, magnetic look directly into the lens.

Pose matches the sign's energy: a warrior-ready stance (Aries); reclining amid abundance (Taurus); a dynamic twisting pose (Gemini); a protective, curled pose in moonlight (Cancer); an arms-wide regal stance (Leo); a precise, poised stance (Virgo); a graceful balanced pose (Libra); a slow, magnetic reclining pose (Scorpio); an adventurous stride or reach toward the horizon (Sagittarius); a commanding stance atop a high vantage point (Capricorn); a sharp architectural pose (Aquarius); a flowing, dreamlike pose (Pisces).

Camera and lighting matched to the sign's element: hard dramatic firelight and lens flare for Fire signs; rich golden-hour or grounded warm light for Earth signs; crisp, high-contrast light for Air signs; soft moonlit or underwater-toned light for Water signs. Shot on a Canon PowerShot G7X or Sony a7III. Vary framing — close beauty shot, waist-up, full-body environmental — independent of which sign came up.

NEVER REPEAT

Beyond the seeded sign selection, treat hair, makeup detail, nails, outfit, symbol placement, exact scene dressing, pose, expression, camera, and lighting as their own independent draws, so even a repeated sign never produces the same photo twice.

HARD LIMITS

One photograph, one woman — no collage, no duplicate figures. No devil, demon, or exorcism imagery — Scorpio's darkness stays sultry and mysterious, never occult. No physically impossible anatomy. No CGI, plastic, or waxy-looking skin, fabric, or environmental effects. Never make her body thin, petite, or straight-bodied — the curvy body lock always applies, including in reclining and curled poses. Face and body stay identical to the locks above in every image; only sign, styling, scene, pose, and lighting are allowed to vary.
