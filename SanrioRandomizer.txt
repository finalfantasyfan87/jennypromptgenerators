**SANRIO WORLD — "SHE'S THE MAIN CHARACTER" RANDOMIZER**

**THE CONCEPT**

Create a hyper-cute, pastel Sanrio-inspired photoshoot where she is the star, and the entire scene — set, props, giant plush mascots — is built around one specific Sanrio character's theme, staged like it all exists to adore her. Photoreal skin and fabric rendered inside a stylized, saturated Sanrio-cartoon world. Vibe: sweet, playful, kawaii, confident — she owns the shoot.

**RANDOM DRAW (run first, don't skip)**

Pick one Sanrio character to theme this shoot around — draw from the full roster: Hello Kitty, My Melody, Kuromi, Cinnamoroll, Pompompurin, Keroppi, Badtz-Maru, Chococat, Little Twin Stars, Pochacco, or Gudetama. Make it a fresh, independent pick with no memory of anything generated earlier in this chat, and actively resist defaulting to the same one or two characters every time — rotate through the full roster over repeated generations. Once picked, every other choice in this prompt (palette, set, hair, makeup, nails, outfit) should key off that character's actual color palette and signature motifs.

**WHO SHE IS — LOCKED**

Her face, bone structure, skin color/tone, and natural hair texture (e.g. curl pattern) come only from the attached reference photo — do not alter, idealize, "fix," or blend her face with any other look. The hairstyle can change with each generation; the texture of the hair never does. Expression is not locked here (see photography section). Body is fixed, not taken from the reference photo: an hourglass figure with a 44DD bust, a 22-inch whittled waist, and 44-inch curvy hips and butt — full and shapely, naturally proportioned, identical across every generation. She is normal human size and scale, with real human skin and body proportions throughout — never shrunk to any doll/chibi size and never rendered with plastic or cartoon body construction. Any oversized scale in this world belongs only to the surrounding props and mascots, never to her.

**WHAT NEVER LOOKS FAKE**

Real skin texture with visible pores and natural subsurface scattering, individual hair strands with natural flyaways even in sleek styles, fabric with real weave/weight/wrinkle behavior, and true-to-life shadow and reflection physics — even though the world around her is exaggerated and cartoon-glossy, she herself must read as a real photographed person dropped into that world, at normal human scale.

High-gloss, high-saturation lighting like a toy commercial crossed with a fashion ad.
